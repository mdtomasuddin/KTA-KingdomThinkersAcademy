1. ROLE IDENTITY & ACCESS BOUNDARIES:
- The Parent/Guardian is the central legal and billing identity.
- Full CRUD authority over child learner sub-accounts, personal profile, and safeguarding consent.
- Exclusive financial authority: Stripe fiat subscriptions, token wallet purchases, ESA reimbursement claims.
- Boundary: Zero access to other families' billing details or unapproved communications.

2. PREVIOUS BACKEND DEVELOPMENT STATUS (WHAT IS BUILT VS WHAT IS PENDING):
- ALREADY COMPLETED IN BACKEND (API & Migrations):
  * Multi-step Onboarding API: `ParentOnboardingApiController` (`/api/parent/onboarding/draft`, `/send-email-otp`, `/verify-email-otp`, `/submit-onboarding`).
  * Taxonomy APIs: `OnboardingDataApiController` (`/interests`, `/learning-styles`, `/faith-formation-goals`, `/project-interests`, `/pace-preferences`, `/avatars`, `/all-data`).
  * Child Learner Account & Credentials Setup: `LearnerSettingApiController` (`/children/{id}/login-setup/send-otp`, `verify-otp`, `set-password`).
  * Token Wallet & Transactions: `TokenApiController` (`/packages`, `/wallet`, `/purchase`).
  * Marketplace Browsing & Enrolment: `MarketplaceApiController`, `EnrollmentApiController` (`/children`, `/enrollment/check-balance`, `/enrollment/token`).
  * Premium Program Subscriptions: `SubscriptionApiController` (`/parent/subscriptions/checkout`, Stripe Webhook).
  * iProject Requests: `ParentIprojectApiController` (`/iproject/mentors`, `/iproject/request`, `/iproject/requests`).
- PENDING / TO BE IMPLEMENTED FOR THIS MODULE:
  * US ESA Funds Enrollment API: `EsaFundsApiController` (`POST /api/parent/esa/submit`, `GET /api/parent/esa/invoices`) + automated 7-year retention PDF receipt generator.
  * Dynamic Anti-Piracy Watermarking: `WatermarkService` (auto-stamping `"KTA+ • Licensed to [Parent Name] • Order #[ID]"` on downloaded worksheet PDFs).
  * Community Social Feed & Connections: `ParentCommunityApiController` (`/community/posts`, `/parents-all-connections`, `/connection-requests`).
  * AI Outcome Generator API: `AiOutcomeApiController` (`POST /api/ai/generate-outcome`, `GET /api/ai/outcomes-history`).
  * Help & Support Tickets: `SupportApiController` (`POST /api/support/tickets`, `GET /api/support/tickets/{id}/messages`).
