1. MULTI-STEP ONBOARDING (/parent/on-board):
- Country Detection: Automatically senses user geography via IP (`AU` or `US`).
- Step 1 (Create Account): Full Name, Email, Phone, Password, Country.
- Step 2 (Verify): 6-digit email OTP verification.
- Step 3 (Add Children): Multi-child builder:
  * Full Name, Date of Birth, Grade Level, Illustrated Avatar selection.
  * Hobbies & Interests: STEM, Coding, Fine Arts, Robotics, Music, Nature Study.
  * Learning Style: Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic, Reading/Writing.
  * Faith Goals: Christian Discipleship, Biblical Worldview, Character Building, Scripture Memorization.
  * Pace: Self-paced, Structured cohort, Flexible hybrid.
  * Future Aspirations text.
- Step 4 (Identity Check — US Only): COPPA parental identity verification.
- Step 5 (Consent): Legal terms, data privacy, academy safety policies.
- Step 6 (Safety): Digital signature confirmation.
- Draft Saving: Synced to `POST /api/parent/onboarding/draft` at every step.

2. PARENT COMMAND DASHBOARD (/parent/dashboard):
- Child Switcher Dropdown: Located top-left. Instantly switches active child learner, updating all metrics and live schedules.
- Metrics Cards: Active Courses Count, Weekly Lessons Completed, Upcoming Live Sessions, Token Balance.
- Live Timetable: Chronological list of Google Meet/Zoom links with green `Join Class` button.
- Weekly Inquiry Milestone: Progress bars for ongoing KTA inquiry cycles.
- Quick Actions: Buttons to explore marketplace, view enrolled projects, open wallet.
