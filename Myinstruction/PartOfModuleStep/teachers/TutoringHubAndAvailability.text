1. /teacher/dashboard/one-on-one-tutoring (TUTORING ROSTER & SLOTS):
   A. Top Banner & Metric Cards (`TeacherOneOnOneStatCard.jsx`):
      - Card 1: Active Tutees / Sessions (e.g., 8 Active Students).
      - Card 2: Completed Sessions (e.g., 24 Sessions Conducted).
      - Card 3: Total Tutoring Hours Logged (e.g., 36.5 Hours).
   B. Booked Sessions Table:
      - Filter by status: All, Upcoming, Completed, Rescheduled.
      - Student Row Details: Avatar, Student Name (e.g., Jacob Jones, Leslie Alexander, Dianne Russell), Date, Time Slot (e.g., 2:00 PM - 3:30 PM), Status Pill.
      - Action: "View Lesson Details" button -> Routes to `/teacher/dashboard/one-on-one-lesson-details`.
   C. Weekly Availability Scheduler:
      - Slot list displaying Open vs Booked slots.
      - Action Button: "Manage Available Time Slots" -> Opens `CtTeacherManageAvailableTimeSlotModal.jsx` allowing the educator to set recurring weekly hours (e.g., Tuesdays & Thursdays 14:00 - 18:00).

2. /teacher/dashboard/one-on-one-lesson-details (PRIVATE SESSION CONSOLE):
   A. Student Header & Quick Connect:
      - Large student avatar, learner grade level, and parent emergency contact info.
      - Primary Actions:
        * "Join Video Call" button (Launches integrated live video room).
        * "Send Message" button (Opens direct message modal).
   B. Student Emotional & Academic Readiness Survey:
      - Pre-session survey completed by student/parent before joining:
        [x] Feeling overwhelmed by schoolwork (Alerts tutor to provide encouragement)
        [ ] Mostly okay, just need some help
        [ ] Feeling confident and ready to stretch
   C. Student Homework & Submissions Inspector:
      - Roster of student uploaded files (e.g., `Week-5-Homework.pdf`, `Math-Problems.jpg`).
      - Visual Actions: Preview in modal (`Eye` icon) or Download original file (`Download` icon).
   D. Assignments Due Tracker:
      - Lists upcoming tests and essays: Title, Due Date, Subject Topic, Priority Badge (`High`, `Medium`).
   E. 4-Phase Structured Session Plan:
      - Phase 1: Connect (5 Min) — Personal check-in, relational connection, prayer.
      - Phase 2: Clarify (10 Min) — Pinpointing areas where the student is stuck.
      - Phase 3: Deep Work (30-40 Min) — Guided instruction and problem solving.
      - Phase 4: Wrap-Up (5 Min) — Summary of key breakthroughs and assigning next steps.
   F. Past Session History:
      - Complete log of all previous meetings with date, duration, and archived tutor notes.

3. PREVIOUS BACKEND WORK VS PENDING IMPLEMENTATION:
   - ALREADY DONE:
     * Tables `tutoring_slots` and `tutoring_bookings` exist in `database.sql`.
   - PENDING IMPLEMENTATION:
     * `TutoringApiController` endpoints:
       1. `GET /api/teacher/tutoring/slots` — Fetch open and booked slots.
       2. `POST /api/teacher/tutoring/slots` — Add recurring availability slots.
       3. `DELETE /api/teacher/tutoring/slots/{id}` — Remove slot.
       4. `GET /api/teacher/tutoring/bookings/{id}` — Get student readiness survey and files.
       5. `POST /api/teacher/tutoring/bookings/{id}/notes` — Save post-session study notes.
