1. INDEPENDENT TEACHER REGISTRATION FLOW:
   A. /teacher/join-now:
      - Introduction hero screen displaying benefits of becoming a KTA+ Educator (Global reach, 70% earnings, faith-aligned community).
      - Action: "Apply as Independent Educator" button -> redirects to `/teacher/create-account`.
   B. /teacher/create-account:
      - Input fields: Full Name, Email Address, Password, Confirm Password, Country selection (Australia, United States, Other).
      - Agreement checkbox: Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
      - Action: "Create Account" -> stores base user in `users` with role `teacher` and status `pending`. Passes `userId` to next step.
   C. /teacher/onboard-details (8-Step Comprehensive Wizard):
      - Top Progress Bar: Dynamic 1-to-8 step indicator with step titles and completion status.
      - Step 1: Personal Details (`TeacherDetailsStep`):
        * Fields: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth (`dob`), Phone Number, Address Line 1, City, State/Province, Postal Code, Country.
        * Biography (rich text), Profile Photo upload (2MB chunked upload).
      - Step 2: Identity Verification (`IdentityStep`):
        * Government Photo ID Type: Passport, Driver's License, National ID Card.
        * ID Number, Expiry Date.
        * Document File Upload (front and back) via 2MB chunked upload (`UploadApiController`).
      - Step 3: Child Safety Verification (`ChildSafetyStep`):
        * Australia: Working With Children Check (WWCC) Registration Number, State Jurisdiction (NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, NT), Expiry Date.
        * United States: State Background Check Confirmation Number / Social Security-linked clearance ID.
        * File upload of official physical card or certificate.
      - Step 4: Child Protection Training (`TrainingStep`):
        * Upload of Child Safeguarding Certification or confirmation of completing the KTA+ Child Protection Induction.
      - Step 5: Professional References (`ReferencesStep`):
        * Two professional/character references: Full Name, Organization, Role/Title, Email Address, Phone Number, Relationship to Applicant.
      - Step 6: Content Specialty & Teaching Sample (`ContentStep`):
        * Primary Subjects (Mathematics, Science, History, Language Arts, Biblical Worldview, etc.).
        * Target Student Age Range (e.g., 6-9, 10-14, 15-18).
        * Sample Lesson Demonstration: Video file upload (chunked) or Vimeo/YouTube video link.
      - Step 7: Legal Agreements (`LegalStep`):
        * Mandatory Checkboxes:
          [x] I have read and agree to the KTA+ Educator Code of Conduct.
          [x] I affirm the Statement of Faith and Educational Philosophy.
          [x] I consent to ongoing background check verification and safeguarding audits.
        * Digital Signature: Full typed legal name and timestamp.
      - Step 8: Final Review & Submission (`ReviewStep`):
        * Full read-only summary of Steps 1 through 7 with quick "Edit" buttons for each section.
        * Action: "Submit Application" -> calls `POST /api/teacher/onboarding/submit-marketplace`.
   D. /teacher/onboard-success:
      - Confirmation screen: "Your application has been submitted for review!"
      - Explains the 24-48 hour compliance screening process.
      - Link to preview Teacher Dashboard in restricted view.

2. CONTRACTED TEACHER REGISTRATION FLOW:
   A. /contracted-teacher/contracted-teacher-onboard (5-Step Specialized Wizard):
      - Step 1: Personal Details (`CT_PersonalDetailsStep`):
        * Full Name, DOB, Address, Phone, Subject specialties, Resume/CV chunked upload (`resume_file`).
      - Step 2: Identity Verification (`CT_IdentityVerificationStep`):
        * Government Photo ID, ID Number, Expiry Date, WWCC verification details.
      - Step 3: Training & Induction (`CT_TrainingStep`):
        * Read KTA Program-Specific Induction modules; digital signature agreeing to curriculum fidelity.
      - Step 4: Mock Teaching Session (`CT_MockSessionStep`):
        * Session Type selection: Live interactive audition or recorded teaching video submission.
        * If live: Pick date, slot, subject, year level, and attach lesson plan.
        * If recorded: Upload lesson video file (`video_file`) via chunked uploader.
      - Step 5: Contract & Terms (`CT_ContractStep`):
        * Independent Contractor Agreement with TTN LLC / KTA Group.
        * Hourly teaching rates, confidentiality, student non-solicitation, and digital signature.
        * Action: "Submit Contracted Educator Application" -> calls `POST /api/teacher/onboarding/submit-contracted`.

3. PREVIOUS BACKEND WORK VS PENDING IMPLEMENTATION:
   - ALREADY DONE:
     * `TeacherOnboardingApiController` endpoints (`/register`, `/submit-contracted`, `/submit-marketplace`).
     * `UploadApiController` chunked upload handler (`/api/upload-chunk`).
     * `users` and `educator_profiles` base database storage.
   - PENDING TASKS:
