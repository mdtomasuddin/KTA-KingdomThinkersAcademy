1. THREE EDUCATOR PERSONAS & DOMAINS:
1. Independent Marketplace Teacher: Authors & sells Group Live, Self-Paced, and 1-on-1 Tutoring courses. Receives 70% net revenue via Stripe Connect; platform keeps 30%.
2. Contracted Teacher (CT): Hired by TTN LLC / KTA Group to teach assigned KTA Explorer/Adventure/Journey cohorts and Thinkers tutoring blocks. Receives contracted compensation based on submitted timesheets.
3. Admin Teacher: Special curriculum author appointed by Super Admin. Authors master KTA inquiry programs and configures subscription pricing plans.

2. PREVIOUS BACKEND DEVELOPMENT STATUS (WHAT IS BUILT VS WHAT IS PENDING):
- ALREADY COMPLETED IN BACKEND (API & Migrations):
  * Teacher Registration: `TeacherOnboardingApiController` (`/register`, `/submit-contracted`, `/submit-marketplace`).
  * 4-Tier Course Builder APIs: `CourseApiController`, `CourseModuleApiController`, `CourseLessonApiController`, `LessonBlockApiController` (`/teacher/courses/*`).
  * Self-Paced Cloning API: `POST /api/teacher/courses/{course_id}/clone-self-paced`.
  * Chunked Video/File Uploads: `UploadApiController` (`POST /api/upload-chunk`).
  * Master Premium Programs & Subscription Plans: `PremiumProgramApiController` (`/teacher/premium-programs/*`).
  * Course Announcements: `CourseDashboardApiController` (`/announcements/*`).
  * Standalone Educator Resources: `TeacherResourceApiController` (`/teacher/resources/*`).
  * Teacher iProject Requests Review: `TeacherIprojectApiController`.
- PENDING / TO BE IMPLEMENTED FOR THIS MODULE:
  * Teacher Safeguarding Compliance Hub API: `TeacherComplianceApiController` (`POST /api/teacher/compliance/submit`, `GET /api/teacher/compliance/status`) — storing WWCC (AU) or Background Check (US), status `pending`, `verified`, `rejected`, expiry alert, and enforcement gate (locking publishing if not verified).
  * Automated 70/30 Stripe Connect Payouts: `TeacherPayoutApiController` (`/api/teacher/payouts/stripe-connect`, `/api/teacher/payouts/ledger`) — Stripe Connect Express onboarding, sales ledger, payout batching.
  * Student Submission Grading API: `TeacherGradingApiController` (`GET /api/teacher/submissions`, `POST /api/teacher/submissions/{id}/grade` — Approve / Request Revision with written feedback).
  * 1-on-1 Tutoring Booking Management: `TutoringApiController` (`/api/teacher/tutoring/slots`, `/api/teacher/tutoring/bookings`, `/session-notes`).
  * Contracted Teacher Timesheet Logger: `ContractedTimesheetApiController` (`POST /api/contracted-teacher/timesheets/log`, `GET /api/contracted-teacher/timesheets/history`).
