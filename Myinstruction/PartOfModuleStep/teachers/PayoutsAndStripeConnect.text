1. SCREEN PURPOSE & FINANCIAL LEDGER:
   This screen manages the financial settlements, revenue sharing, and banking integrations for independent marketplace educators.

2. COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE:
   A. Top Statistics Cards (`TeacherPayoutsTopStats.jsx`):
      - Card 1: Total Earnings (e.g., "$47,320.00" — Lifetime revenue).
      - Card 2: Available to Withdraw (e.g., "$12,453.00" — Cleared funds ready for bank transfer) with quick action button "Request Payout".
      - Card 3: Pending Earnings (e.g., "$12,453.00" — Funds in standard 7-day course completion hold).
      - Card 4: Next Automated Payout Date (e.g., "16 Dec 2025" — Bi-weekly scheduled transfer).
      - Header Action: "View Full Statements & Tax" button -> Opens monthly and annual tax statements.

   B. 70/30 Revenue Share Protocol (`TeacherPayoutsRevenueShare.jsx`):
      - Clear visual breakdown of the marketplace financial split:
        * 70% Net Educator Share: Directly transferred to educator's verified bank account.
        * 30% Platform Cut: Covers payment gateway fees, child safeguarding audits, live video streaming infrastructure, and platform maintenance.
      - Stripe Connect Express Onboarding:
        * If unlinked: Primary call-to-action button "Connect with Stripe" triggering Stripe Express OAuth onboarding.
        * If linked: Displays linked bank status ("Stripe Connected") with direct link to manage bank account or debit card.

   C. Recent Transactions Ledger (`TeacherPayoutsRecentTransaction.jsx`):
      - Table Columns:
        1. Date & Time.
        2. Customer / Student Name.
        3. Course / Resource Title.
        4. Gross Amount ($ USD/AUD or Tokens).
        5. Net Educator Share (70%).
        6. Transfer Status (`Paid`, `Processing`, `Transferred`).

   D. Statements & Tax Console (`TeacherViewStatatements.jsx`):
      - Downloadable PDF earnings statements for monthly reconciliation.
      - End-of-Financial-Year (EOFY for AU) and Form 1099-K (for US) compliant tax reporting documents.

3. PREVIOUS BACKEND WORK VS PENDING IMPLEMENTATION:
   - ALREADY DONE:
     * Table `educator_payout_ledgers` exists in `database.sql` with columns `gross_amount`, `platform_fee`, `net_amount`, `stripe_transfer_id`.
   - PENDING IMPLEMENTATION:
     * Stripe Connect Express webhook integration (`/api/teacher/payouts/stripe-connect/callback`).
     * `TeacherPayoutApiController` endpoints:
       1. `GET /api/teacher/payouts/summary` — Aggregates balance, available, pending.
       2. `GET /api/teacher/payouts/transactions` — Paginated sales ledger.
       3. `POST /api/teacher/payouts/request-transfer` — Triggers instant Stripe payout.
       4. `GET /api/teacher/payouts/statements/{month}` — Generates PDF invoice breakdown.
