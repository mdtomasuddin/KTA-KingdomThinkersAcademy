1. ONBOARDING SEQUENCE & WORKFLOW:
The Co-Op onboarding wizard guides prospective pod leaders through organizational profile creation, tiered SaaS subscription selection, and compliance agreement before administrative review.

2. DETAILED STEPS:
- Intro Page (/co-op/onboarding): Explains pod benefits, group licensing economies, and platform features. Action: Button `Get Started`.
- Plan Selection (/co-op/onboarding/plan):
  * Tier 1: Seedling (Up to 15 students) — $39/month or $390/year.
  * Tier 2: Community (Up to 40 students) — $89/month or $890/year.
  * Tier 3: Network (Unlimited students / multi-pod) — $199/month.
  * Action: Stripe payment checkout for organization subscription.
- Form Step 1 — Basic Details (BasicDetailsStep.jsx):
  * Fields: Co-op Name, City & Region, Physical Meeting Address, Pod Description, Country selection (AU or US).
- Form Step 2 — Visibility (VisibilityStep.jsx):
  * 3 Radio Options:
    1. Public: Listed on KTA+ Co-op Directory for local discovery.
    2. Unlisted: Accessible only via direct coordinator share link.
    3. Invite-Only: Completely hidden; requires direct email invitation.
- Form Step 3 — Age Range (AgeRangeStep.jsx):
  * Checkboxes for target grade bands: Early Primary (Grades K-2), Primary (Grades 3-5), Middle School (Grades 6-8), High School (Grades 9-12).
- Form Step 4 — Agreements (AgreementsStep.jsx):
  * Checkboxes: Agree to Community Guidelines, Child Safeguarding Standards, and Platform Terms.
- Form Step 5 — Review & Submit (ReviewStep.jsx):
  * Summary cards displaying all entered details with `Edit` shortcuts. Button `Submit Application` ➔ directs to `/co-op/onboarding/under-review`.
