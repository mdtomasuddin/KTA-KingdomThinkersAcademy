-----------------
1. DASHBOARD & TIMETABLE:
- Weekly Checklist (/student/enrolled): To-do items, upcoming deadlines, direct lesson launcher.
- Visual Timetable (/student/schedule): Google Meet / Zoom join links (activates 10 mins prior to class).

2. THE STUDENT HIVE & PORTFOLIO:
- The Student Hive (/student/hive): Moderated peer activity stream to share project milestones with classmates.
- Portfolio & Badges Locker (/student/profile): Displays earned digital badges (e.g. "Scripture Scholar", "STEM Innovator"), avatar, and finished iProjects.

3. 4-TIER LMS PLAYER & SUBMISSIONS:
- Lesson Player (/student/view-kta-lesson): Slide reader, chunked video playback, formatted scripture text, instant-scoring quiz blocks.
- Assignment Submission (/student/lesson-submit-blocks): Text reflection input and photo/document file uploader.
- Study Journal (/student/notes): Personal notes editor and read-only inspection of post-session tutor remarks.
