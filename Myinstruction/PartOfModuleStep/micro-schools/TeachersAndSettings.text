[ENGLISH SECTION]
-----------------
1. TEACHERS MANAGEMENT:
- Faculty Directory: Employed school educators, compliance verification status badge (`Verified`), active classroom count, weekly teaching hours.
- Teacher Details: Assigned class instances, teaching timetable, performance reviews.
- Pending Teachers: Review incoming teacher applications or send direct email invitations. Action: Button `Approve Educator`.

2. MESSAGING & ANNOUNCEMENTS:
- Channels: Staff room (`#teachers`), Parent notices (`#families`), direct messaging.
- Bulletins: Publish school closures, event reminders, and term calendars.

3. SETTINGS & CAMPUS BRANDING:
- Campus Info: Legal school name, physical address, contact phone, operating format.
- Subdomain: Custom school portal access URL: `[school].ktaplus.io`.
- Branding: School logo and header banner upload.
- Subscription: Current plan tier, active vs. available seat count, billing invoice history.

২. নোটিশ ও মেসেজিং:
