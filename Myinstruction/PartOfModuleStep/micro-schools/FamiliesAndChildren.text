[ENGLISH SECTION]
-----------------
1. FAMILIES MANAGEMENT:
- Directory: Searchable list of registered parent households, contacts, and enrolled children count.
- Details View: Guardian identity, emergency contact verification, list of affiliated students.
- Pending Families: Review applications from parents seeking school affiliation. Action: Button `Approve` or `Decline`.

2. CHILDREN / STUDENT BODY ROSTER:
- Directory: Roster of all enrolled students, assigned class instances, grade levels, and attendance rates.
- Academic Details View: Child profile, enrolled classes, grades ledger, daily attendance, health/dietary alerts.
- Pending Child Approvals: Review student seat additions submitted by admitted families. Action: Button `Approve` or `Reject`.
