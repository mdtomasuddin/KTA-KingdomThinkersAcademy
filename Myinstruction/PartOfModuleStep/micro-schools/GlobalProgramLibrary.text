[ENGLISH SECTION]
-----------------
1. CURRICULUM FEED (/micro-school/dashboard/global-program-library):
- Displays all available KTA+ Premium Programs (automatically included under the school B2B plan) and licensed marketplace courses.
- Filter by Subject, Grade Band, and Enablement Status (`All`, `Enabled`, `Disabled`).

2. ONE-CLICK ENABLE / DISABLE TOGGLE:
- School administrator switches the toggle to "Enabled" to expose the course in the `Create Class Instance` dropdown.
- Disabling a program preserves past class archives while preventing new class scheduling.

3. STRICT READ-ONLY CURRICULUM SAFEGUARD:
- When viewing `/micro-school/dashboard/program-details-page`, a prominent yellow banner displays:
  "Central Curriculum — Read-Only Mode. Curriculum content is centrally managed by KTA+ to ensure accreditation and academic standards."
- Local staff cannot alter lesson text, remove blocks, or edit quiz criteria.
